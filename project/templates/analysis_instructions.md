# How to write a job-fit analysis

Write to me directly, in plain language. Be concise and specific. Do not add cheerleading or a cover letter.

## Sections, in this order

1. **What fits** — strong matches first, then partial matches. One or two sentences per requirement.
2. **What doesn't fit** — gaps. List required gaps before preferred gaps.
3. **Add to your resume** — only experience that is already saved and is not on the resume. Cite the experience ID.
4. **Questions for you** — at most 3 short questions about experience I might have that is not in the database. Use plain, everyday words: one short sentence each, asking one thing, that I can answer in a few words.

## Rules

- Every match cites one requirement ID (REQ-…) and at least one experience ID (EXP-…).
- Do not invent experience, employers, or outcomes.
- If something is missing from the database, ask a question. Do not suggest adding it as if it were a fact.
- Keep each explanation to one or two sentences.
